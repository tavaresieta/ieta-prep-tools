Pillar 1 — Brazil Regulatory Watch

Tax reform leaves carbon credit treatment unresolved, generating legal uncertainty for SBCE operators

Legal analysis published this week notes that Brazil's consumption tax reform legislation does not establish a specific regime for carbon assets nor clarify their legal nature for the incidence of the new CBS and IBS taxes. The exemption from PIS and Cofins granted under Law 15,042/2024 to CBEs, CRVEs, and other SBCE instruments was not explicitly reproduced for CBS, raising litigation risk as the pre-operational phase advances.

Mauro Negruni [PT], 23 July 2026

Fenafisco warns lack of clarity places carbon credits in tax reform crosshairs

The National Federation of State Tax Auditors argues that without a targeted rule on CBIO, CRVE, and voluntary market credits under the new CBS and IBS framework, tax authorities and market participants face conflicting interpretations on trading, retirement, and cross-border transfer events. The note calls for a Receita Federal instrução normativa before obligatory filling of the new tax fields becomes mandatory on 3 August.

Fenafisco [PT], 21 July 2026

Interministerial working group advances rules for carbon credit projects on federal public lands

The Ministries of Finance and Environment and Climate Change publicised the working group tasked with defining guidelines for carbon credit projects on federal public lands, covering conservation units, indigenous territories, rural settlements, and unallocated public forests. The group has 60 days to present a diagnosis and recommendations covering land tenure, credit ownership, and the safeguards owed to indigenous peoples, traditional communities, and family farmers.

XP Café com ESG [PT], 21 July 2026

MMA public consultation on ITMO regulation continues to receive contributions ahead of 6 August deadline

The Ministry of Environment and Climate Change's public consultation on the joint SEMC-SMC-SEPPE-SECLIMA draft resolution regulating Internationally Transferred Mitigation Outcomes remains open on the Brasil Participativo platform. The draft caps international transfers at up to 50 million tonnes of CO2e over 2031-2035, contingent on generation of CRVE-compatible credits and prior authorisation by the Designated National Authority.

Brasil Participativo [PT], consultation open through 6 August 2026

Pillar 2 — Article 6 & International Mechanisms

German government advisors call for centralised Article 6 procurement inside the EU ETS

A discussion paper published by the German Environment Agency (UBA) argues that international carbon credits under Article 6 of the Paris Agreement should only be integrated into the EU Emissions Trading System through a centrally managed purchasing mechanism, rather than allowing regulated entities to buy offsets directly for compliance. The paper feeds into the debate on how the European Commission's 17 July ETS revision proposal will handle the international credit channel from 2036 onwards. For IETA, the discussion sharpens the design question over how any centralised Article 6 procurement would interface with host-country registries such as those Brazil is building.

Carbon Pulse [EN], 24 July 2026 (summary access only)

Analysis: EU ETS reform proposals suggest Article 6 demand could reach 650 Mt by 2040

Fresh modelling of the European Commission's 17 July revision package indicates that total EU demand for Article 6 units could climb to approximately 650 million tonnes by 2040, once the 5% net-emissions ceiling operational from 2036 is combined with the earlier procurement pathway proposed for the 2040 climate target. The projection would materially reshape demand-side signals for host countries authorising ITMO exports, including Brazil.

Carbon Pulse [EN], 21 July 2026 (summary access only)

Resistance builds in European Parliament to inclusion of international carbon credits and removals in EU ETS

Members of the European Parliament are signalling opposition to the Commission's plan to allow Article 6 international credits and permanent removals to count towards ETS compliance obligations, even through the indirect institutional channel proposed. The trilogue path for the Phase 5 legal framework covering 2031-2040 now looks contested, which extends uncertainty for potential host-country suppliers negotiating bilateral arrangements.

Carbon Pulse [EN], 21 July 2026 (summary access only)

Pillar 3 — Market Integrity, Standards & Global Compliance

Open interest on the CORSIA carbon futures curve breaches 1 million units

Open interest across CORSIA-eligible carbon futures listed on Abaxx Exchange and ICE has passed one million units for the first time, following the European Commission's proposal to extend EU ETS coverage to some flights beyond the European Economic Area from 2029. Abaxx also reported June 2026 as its highest-volume month to date, with average daily open interest up roughly 200% over May.

Carbon Pulse [EN], 22 July 2026 (summary access only)

EU allowance prices settle at EUR 83.88/t as market digests ETS revision proposal

EU Emission Allowance benchmark prices closed at EUR 83.88 per tonne on 24 July, marking a modest daily gain and holding well above the EUR 80 level maintained through the second half of the month. Traders continue to weigh the Commission's 17 July revision package, which sets a slower cap reduction trajectory of 3.7% during 2031-2035 and 1.7% from 2036, alongside extended free allocations for exposed industrial sectors.

Trading Economics [EN], 24 July 2026

Study finds carbon credit prices reflect buyer identity more than delivered mitigation

A new academic study reported this week concludes that voluntary carbon credit prices in secondary market transactions are driven more strongly by the identity and reputation of the buyer than by the underlying quality of the mitigation delivered. The finding is likely to sharpen scrutiny of ratings-based price discovery and inform ongoing IC-VCM and VCMI discussions on how integrity signals are transmitted to the price curve.

Carbon Pulse [EN], 21 July 2026 (summary access only)

Pillar 4 — Corporate Demand, Trade, Aviation & IETA Members

IC-VCM closes applications for Asia-Pacific Indigenous Peoples and Local Communities Governing Board seat

The Integrity Council for the Voluntary Carbon Market closed applications on 24 July for one of the three Governing Board seats reserved for Indigenous Peoples and local community leaders. The successful Asia-Pacific candidate will hold full voting rights over Core Carbon Principles decisions and wider VCM governance, directly shaping the CCP labelling that determines demand for methodologies used by Brazilian VCM projects.

IC-VCM [EN], deadline 24 July 2026

ART hosts French and Portuguese language webinars on TREES 3.0 for jurisdictional REDD+ programmes

The Architecture for REDD+ Transactions is running its multilingual roll-out of the TREES 3.0 standard this week, with a French-language webinar on 23 July and a Portuguese-language session scheduled for 30 July. TREES 3.0 opens new pathways for jurisdictions to join and streamlines requirements, and directly underpins the LEAF Coalition and COP30 Scaling J-REDD+ Coalition targeting USD 3-6 billion per year by 2030, with several Brazilian states positioned to participate.

ART [EN], webinar series 22-30 July 2026

ICAO confirms ACR jurisdictional power sector programme as CORSIA-eligible in global first for sectoral crediting

The International Civil Aviation Organization has affirmed that ACR's Standard for the Transformation of the Electric Power Sector (STEPS) qualifies as a CORSIA-eligible programme, marking the first time a jurisdictional sectoral crediting approach has been recognised under the aviation scheme. STEPS was developed alongside the Energy Transition Accelerator initiative and expands the supply channel available to airlines facing rising CORSIA compliance costs.
