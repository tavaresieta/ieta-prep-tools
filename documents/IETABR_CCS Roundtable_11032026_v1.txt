MRV Benchmarks and Reversal Risk
Brazil's MRV Institutional Structure:
▪The Sub-Secretariat for Implementation holds the mandate to develop MRV norms for all regulated activities 
and emission sources. Responsibilities include drafting sector -specific monitoring protocols ; overseeing 
compliance with reporting obligations; auditing verification bodies
▪Year-one priorities: MRV rules finalized, operators prepared, and a reporting system prototype under testing .
▪The MRV process follows three mandatory steps:
1.Monitoring Plan Operators submit a monitoring plan for each commitment period , subject to prior approval by the 
governing body, following prescribed models and deadlines
2.Annual Emissions Report Operators submit annual GHG emissions and removals reports in accordance with the 
approved monitoring plan
3.Central Registry Validated data is entered into the SBCE Central Registry in each operator's dedicated account

The road ahead: price signals from the SBCE and next steps
SBCE Phase Triggers 
•Phase II opens the pilot window: voluntary reporting, no compliance penalty, ideal timing to establish 
operational baselines and submit methodology drafts to the governing body
•Phase III creates real demand: mandatory MRV and compliance obligations
•The critical trigger is the governing body's definition of sectoral caps: without a cap, there is no scarcity, and 
without scarcity there is no price signal strong enough to justify CAPEX -intensive projects (BECCS, DAC, 
but also ARR)
The Carbon Price Signal -Viability Threshold s:
•The SBCE has not yet defined a penalty rate or price floor. BECCS and DAC require either a significantly 
higher price or complementary policy support. 
•Price predictability over a 10 –15 year horizon matters as much as price level, investors need policy 
durability, not just a favourable spot price .
