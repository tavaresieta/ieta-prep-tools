Pillar 1 — Brazil Regulatory Watch

Lula reopens UN General Assembly with call to place climate action back at the centre of the international agenda

President Luiz Inácio Lula da Silva delivered Brazil's traditional opening speech at the 80th UN General Assembly on 22 September, criticising the weakening of climate ambition inside the G7 and G20 and calling for a coordinated pushback against climate denialism. Lula reaffirmed that Brazil will advance the roadmap for decarbonising its economy and framed the country's natural-resource strategy as compatible with a low-carbon transition, a message that reinforces the political backing for the ongoing implementation of the Sistema Brasileiro de Comércio de Emissões (SBCE). Brazil advances federal Amazon concession pipeline targeting 36,000 hectares of restoration linked to carbon-credit revenue

Brazil has moved forward with a new tranche of federal concessions covering roughly 36,000 hectares of Amazon forest to be restored under contracts that can generate carbon credits. According to Carbon Pulse, one concession is already at tender stage while a second remains under public consultation, extending the model piloted with the earlier Bom Futuro award. For IETA members monitoring supply channels into the future SBCE and the voluntary market, this is a key indicator of how Brazil intends to interface public-land restoration with credit issuance ahead of finalising the SBCE-VCM integration rules. Pillar 3 — Market Integrity, Standards & Global Compliance

EU ETS allowance benchmark slips to €86.18 on 22 September as broader energy complex weakens

The December EU Allowance benchmark closed at €86.18/t on 22 September, down 0.45% on the day and marking a second consecutive session of losses, according to Trading Economics data. The move comes as market participants weigh the European Commission's July proposal to revise the EU ETS for Phase 5 (2031–2040), which remains under Council and Parliament scrutiny and is expected to shape the auction and free-allocation profile relevant to Brazilian exporters under CBAM. [EN]
